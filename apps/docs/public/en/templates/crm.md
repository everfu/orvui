# CRM

A sales workspace combining date ranges, funnels, forecasts, accounts and opportunities.

<a id="template-use-cases"></a>
### When to use it
For accounts, opportunities, contacts and team follow-up management.

<a id="preview"></a>
## Interactive preview
[Live preview](https://orvui-template-crm.netlify.app?lang=en)

<a id="template-walkthrough"></a>
### Try it
1. Search for Northstar in Accounts, open its detail and edit the record.
2. Follow related records to contacts and opportunities, then update an opportunity stage.
3. Complete tasks, record meetings or notes, and save list filters.

<a id="notes"></a>
## Usage notes
Supports Chinese, English, light and dark appearance and eleven palettes. Demo data stays in this browser; changing language preserves edits. Reset demo in Settings restores the sample data.

Mail, AI responses and financial actions only update local state. Live demos are public; complete sources are maintained in private repositories.
